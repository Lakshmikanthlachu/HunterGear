Why hunters shop here

We’re a small shop with a short list of rules: test it, fit it, explain it, and take it back if it’s wrong.

  • Tested on real hunts

    Staff wear every boot, pack and jacket in the field before it earns shelf space.

  • Fitted before you buy

    Bring your boots, bow or pack. We size, adjust and tune in store at no charge.

  • Straight answers

    Ask about weight, warmth or range and get a plain reply, even if it points you to the cheaper option.

  • 30-day returns

    Wear it around the house. If it is not right, bring it back within 30 days.

Hunt legal. Hunt visible. Tell someone where you’re going.

  • Carry your licence and the right tag for every animal you plan to take.
  • Wear blaze orange wherever it’s required, and where it isn’t, wear it anyway.
  • Share your location and return time before you leave the truck.
